# Lab Assessments

> Graded practical checks that map one-to-one onto the module labs
> (`modules/*/content/labs/`). Each sheet is a *reduced* version of
> the corresponding lab: fewer steps, tighter time, graded strictly
> on evidence. Students who did the lab honestly will find nothing
> new here — that is the point: it verifies practice, not cramming.

## Format (all sheets)

- 30–45 minutes, in-lab session, instructor observes + spot-checks
  the terminal.
- Deliverable: end state + a 5–10 line evidence transcript
  (`script lab-check.log`).
- Grading: each sheet has a 10-point rubric; **method** points are
  separable from **result** points (result-only caps at 6/10).

## Relationship to other assessments

- Sheets are **formative-adjacent**: low stakes (10% of course grade
  total, 2 points each), high signal. A student failing a sheet
  should be routed to the corresponding module lab before the
  practical exam.
- The practical exam ([../practical/practical-exam.md](../practical/practical-exam.md))
  reuses these fault styles at exam scale with planted snapshots.
