## Generic analytic rubric (any Linux practical artifact)

Four dimensions × four levels. Weight to taste; state the weights on
the assessment itself.

| Dimension | 4 — Excellent | 3 — Proficient | 2 — Developing | 1 — Beginning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Correctness of end state** | State matches spec exactly; verified independently | Matches spec; verification partial | Minor deviations; unverified | Not achieved or check gamed |
| **Evidence-based diagnosis** | Every claim cites a command/output line | Mostly cited; one gap | Diagnosis plausible, evidence thin | Assertion without evidence |
| **Safety & blast radius** | Least-privilege throughout; blast radius stated unprompted | Safe; blast radius on request | One unsafe-but-recovered step | Unsafe or unaware |
| **Communication** | Another admin could replay from the notes alone | Complete but terse | Missing cause-or-fix section | Notes absent/unusable |

**Grade capture:** use the
[generic grading sheet](../../instructor-resources/grading-sheets/lab-assessment-sheet.md)
🔒 — it applies these dimensions with per-point evidence quoting.

## Second-mark policy (course-wide)

Second-mark whenever: total ≥ 85%, any zero-score item, any integrity
flag, or a student dispute. The second marker sees the artifact +
transcript + first marker's evidence quotes, and marks independently.
