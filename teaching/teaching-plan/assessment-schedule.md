## Cadence principles

1. **Nothing graded in weeks 1–2** — environment success first; graded
   pressure too early poisons lab culture.
2. **One major instrument per week maximum** — quizzes are weekly and
   light; LA circuits, assignments, and exams alternate so no week
   carries two heavy submissions.
3. **Every instrument exists already** — this schedule only places the
   repository's own artifacts on a calendar; it creates no new graded
   item (the [teaching-package quiz bank v2](../assessments/README.md)
   and A4–A6 are *optional replacements/enrichment*, flagged as such).
4. **Keys stay instructor-side** — student-facing pages point at
   instruments, never at their answers.

## If you teach the two-semester variant

Halve the pace: each listed item shifts +7 weeks; add a second
consolidation week before the midterm; the capstone gains a dedicated
*operate* fortnight (its rubric's phase 3 rewards it).
