## Scheduling assumptions (stated up front)

- **16 instructional weeks, 2 sessions/week, 90 min/session** = 32 sessions.
- The 30 numbered modules do not map 1:1 to 32 sessions — the plan uses a
  **2-modules-per-week default rhythm** (which is also the pace stated in
  the README) and spends its spare sessions deliberately: course
  introduction (W1), consolidation before the midterm (W7), revision +
  practical assessment in the final week (W16). Companion modules M31/M32
  are taught *inside* the capstone weeks rather than as separate slots.
- Session 1 assumes **no Linux experience** and no installed VM — its lab
  is environment setup (M04 material), which is why M01–M04 compress into
  the first two weeks with heavier homework.
- Exams are placed where the in-repo papers' scopes put them: midterm
  covers M01–M13 (end of W7), final covers M14–M31 (W16), practical exam
  and capstone defense in W15–W16.
- Weightings follow the repository's documented scheme (quizzes 10%,
  labs 10%, assignments 3×5%, midterm 20%, final 25%, practical 15%,
  capstone rubric) — no invented weightings.

## How to adapt

The plan is a default, not a mandate. Two sanctioned variations are
marked inline: a **1-module/week** two-semester variant (drop the
compression note in W2–W15 and follow the mapping doc's "expanded" column)
and a **practical-heavy variant** for lab-first cohorts (swap lecture/lab
order; marked at each session where the deck's demo doubles as the lab
briefing).
