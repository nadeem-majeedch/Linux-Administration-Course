## How labs are graded (the short version)

- **Module labs** are practice — checked for completion and checkpoint
  answers, not marked in detail
- **LA circuits** (LA-1…LA-5) are graded 10-point practicals — one per
  major unit, scheduled in the [assessment calendar](../teaching-plan/assessment-schedule.md)
- **The Level 1–5 ladder** ([course labs](../../labs/README.md)) is the
  self-paced mastery track — Level 5 is the capstone's proving ground
- Every graded lab requires a **`script` transcript started before any
  work** — end states alone cap below full marks

## The workbook in one paragraph

Read the rules once, use the unit indexes to find each session's lab,
keep your transcripts started-early and your teardowns censused, and
treat every Checkpoint question as the actual assignment — the commands
are the easy part; the *evidence discipline* is what's being trained.
