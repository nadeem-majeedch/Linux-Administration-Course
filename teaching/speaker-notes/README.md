## Structure of every notes file

1. Teaching purpose (what the unit is *for*, in one breath)
2. Opening question with expected answers
3. Per-slide/per-section guidance
4. Terminology to insist on
5. Unit-wide misconceptions
6. Live-demo instructions with error tables
7. Classroom activities
8. Timing and what to cut when running long
9. Transition to the next unit
10. Unit-level instructor checklist

## Relationship to other materials

- The **deck** is what students see; the **notes** are what the
  instructor rehearses; the **module teaching guides**
  ([../instructor-manual/module-teaching-guides/](../instructor-manual/module-teaching-guides/README.md))
  carry the per-module depth (difficult concepts, extension activities).
- Notes deliberately do not repeat command syntax — that lives in the
  modules and the [cheatsheets](../../cheatsheets/README.md).
